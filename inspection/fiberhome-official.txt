LG6121F

                    

LG6121F is an indoor 5G CPE for home. Downlink supports 2.4GHz and 5GHz dual band Wi-Fi 6, maximum speed up to 1800Mbps. Besides using in home, LG6121F is also suitable for camping, picnic, open cinema, and other outdoor scenarios. It supports ethernet cable uplink, so after the fiber is deployed, it can be used as a router to increase the availability of this device.
